# The template process: onboarding, gating, provenance, activation

> **Preview.** The template process is implemented but not yet declared Stable; interfaces and limits
> may change.

Written while the process is built: steps marked _(planned)_ do not exist yet. **Self-serve** (the
template lifecycle design, `docs/specs/2026-11-03-template-lifecycle-and-provenance-design.md`, 0.4):
a client onboards and activates its own templates; policy, not an approver, gates them; admins set
the policy and the quotas.

Each step names who does it, the tool, the rules it applies (`GATING-RULES.md`, beside this page)
and the record it leaves. Provenance is hosted in the control plane; the workload plane (the KVM
host, the worker) only produces it, and every record must be verifiable offline. The last column is
what the units proposed with #730 to #752 implement.

Every enrolled subject is a self-serve client and admins are `SH_ADMIN_SUBJECTS`; the
settings are the bootstrap, and an admin named there enrolls subjects and grants admin through
`/v1/subjects`. The rest of the authorization design comes later.
